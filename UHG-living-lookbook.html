UHG Living Lookbook

Early in 2025, I was asked to develop an alternative to Issuu flip book idea generators for UHG, which were being blocked by the company’s firewall. I created an interactive, non-transactional site to generate ideas and showcase current trends. The solution is fully responsive, highly engaging for users, and provides a modern framework for future updates—all seamlessly integrated within our eStore platform.
