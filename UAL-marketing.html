United Airlines Creative Marketing

As the Art Director for United Airlines at Staples Promotional Products, I have had the privilege of putting together assets for a variety of marketing spaces. Some of these are for email marketing campaigns, some will be used for in-flight screens, and others yet for a variety of digital spaces used by the United Airlines employees. The golden ratio is a large part of the United Airlines brand, so the assets reflect this along with a clean use of their typography and dynamic images.
