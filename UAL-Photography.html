United Airlines Photography Direction

Strong photography is key to an engaging website and effective marketing. For United Airlines, I focused on creating clean, professional imagery that highlighted the merchandise. I directed a photoshoot using dynamic angles, flexible white backgrounds that could shift to United’s color palette, and thoughtfully selected products that stood out. The photo studio in Orange City did an excellent job bringing this vision to life.
