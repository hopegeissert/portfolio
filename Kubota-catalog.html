2022-2023 Kubota Catalog

This was the third catalog I created for Kubota and I really wanted to push myself to think outside the box and come up with creative new imagery. I enlisted the help of local people in the Orange City area to model for different images and photoshopped in backgrounds where needed. This resulted in vibrant new layouts for this catalog.
