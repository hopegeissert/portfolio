Flyers for Inside Sales Team at SPP

I adapt quickly to learn the brand that is being featured, and I put together cohesive and easy-to-digest flyers using the images and information I’m given. These are often quick-turn projects and have really helped out the sales team in distributing their ideas efficiently.
